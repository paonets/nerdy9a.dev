# The Power of Habit: Why We Do What We Do in Life and Business

![image](attachments/the_power_of_habit_sketchnote.webp)

## Summary

_The Power of Habit_ explores the neurological patterns that form habits and how they can be transformed. Key themes from my highlights:

- **The Habit Loop:** Habits consist of a three-step process: a **Cue**, a **Routine**, and a **Reward**. The engine that drives this loop is a neurological **craving**.
- **The Golden Rule of Habit Change:** To change a habit, you must keep the old cue and old reward, but insert a new routine. **Belief** is the essential ingredient that makes these changes permanent.
- **Keystone Habits and Willpower:** Some habits, like willpower, are more important than others because they create "small wins" that spill over into other areas of life. Willpower is described as a **muscle** that can be strengthened but also fatigued.
- **Organizational and Social Habits:** Companies and societies are driven by collective habits. Giving individuals a **sense of agency** can radically improve focus and success.
- **Implementation Intentions:** The most effective way to change behavior is to decide on a routine ahead of time for specific "inflection points" or cues.
