# Same as Ever: Timeless Lessons on Risk, Opportunity and Living a Good Life

![image](attachments/same_as_ever_sketchnote.webp)

## Summary

_Same as Ever_ focuses on the unchanging behaviors and traits of humanity as the most reliable way to navigate an uncertain future. Key themes from my highlights:

- **Focus on Permanence:** Instead of trying to predict _what_ will change, focus on _what never changes_—how people respond to fear, greed, and tribal affiliations.
- **The Equation of Happiness:** Happiness is determined by the gap between what you have and what you expect. **Low expectations** are the "first rule of happiness."
- **Pessimism for Survival, Optimism for Growth:** Long-term success requires being pessimistic enough to survive the short run (cash, low leverage, margin of safety) so you can be an optimist in the long run.
- **Storytelling over Accuracy:** People crave certainty over accuracy. Market valuations and prices are often driven more by the "stories about tomorrow" than the numbers of today.
- **The Danger of Forced Growth:** Rapid or forced growth often backfires. True value comes from patience and allowing things to grow naturally.
- **Efficiency is Vulnerability:** Just as in evolution, trying to be "too perfect" or "too efficient" makes you brittle. A little inefficiency and a margin of safety provide the flexibility needed to endure "short-term nonsense."
