# Atomic Habits: An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones

> [!info] Book summary · by James Clear · read Jul 2023

![image](attachments/atomic_habits_sketchnote.webp)

## Summary

_Atomic Habits_ is a comprehensive guide to behavior change through the power of small, compounding actions. Key themes include:

- **Identity-Based Habits:** Focus on _who_ you want to become rather than _what_ you want to achieve. Every habit is a "vote" for the person you want to be.
- **The 4 Laws of Behavior Change:** A framework to build good habits (Obvious, Attractive, Easy, Satisfying) and break bad ones (Invisible, Unattractive, Difficult, Unsatisfying).
- **Systems Over Goals:** Forget about goals; focus on the systems that lead to them. You fall to the level of your systems.
- **Environment Design:** Discipline is often a result of a disciplined environment. Make the cues for good habits obvious and the cues for bad habits invisible.
- **Practical Strategies:** Use **Habit Stacking**, the **2-Minute Rule**, and **Implementation Intentions** to make starting easier.
- **The Goldilocks Rule:** Maintain motivation by working on tasks of "just manageable difficulty" to achieve flow and avoid boredom.
