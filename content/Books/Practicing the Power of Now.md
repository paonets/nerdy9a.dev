# Practicing the Power of Now

![image](attachments/practicing_the_power_of_now_sketchnote.webp)

## Summary

_Practicing the Power of Now_ is a practical guide to spiritual awakening through the cultivation of presence. Key themes from my highlights:

- **Watching the Thinker:** A central practice is becoming the "witness" of your thoughts. By observing your mind without judgment, you break your identification with it and access the deeper dimension of Being.
- **Inner Body Awareness:** To reclaim consciousness from the mind, Tolle emphasizes directing attention into the body. Feeling the "inner energy field" serves as an anchor in the Now and connects you to your emotions and creative stillness.
- **Clock Time vs. Psychological Time:** Use time for practical matters ("clock time"), but avoid "psychological time"—the compulsive identification with the past and projection into the future, which are described as illusions.
- **The Pain-Body:** Presence is the key to dissolving the "pain-body" (accumulated emotional pain). By observing the pain-body directly as it becomes active, you break your identification with it.
- **Relationships as Sadhana:** Relationships can become a spiritual practice. Complete acceptance of a partner without judgment and maintaining presence in their "unconscious" moments allows for true love relationships.
- **Acceptance and Positive Action:** Surrender is not passive; it involves accepting the "isness" of the present moment without labeling it as "bad." This lack of resistance allows for more effective, "positive action" to change one's circumstances.
