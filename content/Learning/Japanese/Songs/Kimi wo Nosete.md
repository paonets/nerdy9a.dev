# 君をのせて (Carrying You) — Song Study & Lyrics Breakdown

- **Title:** 君をのせて (Kimi wo Nosete — _Carrying You_)
- **Vocal:** 井上あずみ (Azumi Inoue)
- **Lyrics:** 宮崎駿 (Hayao Miyazaki)
- **Music & Arrangement:** 久石譲 (Joe Hisaishi)
- **Theme Song:** Studio Ghibli's _Castle in the Sky_ (天空の城ラピュタ — _Tenkuu no Shiro Laputa_, 1986)

- **Related Studies:** [[Learning/Japanese/Songs/Itsumo Nando Demo|Itsumo Nando Demo]] | [[Learning/Japanese/Songs/Sayonara no Natsu|Sayonara no Natsu]] | [[Learning/Japanese/Songs/Meguru Kisetsu|Meguru Kisetsu]]

## 🌟 Background & Central Themes

Written directly by director **Hayao Miyazaki** himself, _Kimi wo Nosete_ captures the essence of Pazu and Sheeta's journey. Rather than a simple adventure anthem, it balances **solitude, romantic yearning, family inheritance, and cosmic wonder**.

### Core Themes:

1. **Poetic Attribution (`〜のは〜から`)**: Everyday natural phenomena (the glow of the horizon, town lights) are given emotional significance because the person you seek is out there somewhere.
2. **Inheritance of Spirit**: Pazu travels light (bread, knife, lamp), carrying no material wealth, but inherits what truly matters: his father's passionate conviction (`熱い想い`) and his mother's loving gaze (`まなざし`).
3. **The Spinning Earth as a Cradle (`地球はまわる 君をのせて`)**: The Earth itself is a giant vessel spinning through space, carrying two destined young souls toward an inevitable meeting.

## 🎵 Lyrics & Detailed Analysis

### Stanza 1: The Horizon & The Lights of Home

| Japanese                                                                                                                                   | English                                                                                                         |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| あの<ruby>地平線<rt>ちへいせん</rt></ruby>　<ruby>輝<rt>かがや</rt></ruby>くのは<br>どこかに<ruby>君<rt>きみ</rt></ruby>をかくしているから | The reason that horizon shines<br>Is because somewhere it is hiding you                                         |
| たくさんの<ruby>灯<rt>ひ</rt></ruby>がなつかしいのは<br>あのどれかひとつに　<ruby>君<rt>きみ</rt></ruby>がいるから                         | The reason all those countless lights feel so nostalgic<br>Is because you are there, living beneath one of them |

#### 🔑 Vocab & Points

- **<ruby>地平線<rt>ちへいせん</rt></ruby> (chiheisen):** Horizon (land horizon). Notice: sea horizon is <ruby>水平線<rt>すいへいせん</rt></ruby> (_suiheisen_).
- **<ruby>輝<rt>かがや</rt></ruby>く (kagayaku):** To shine, sparkle, or glow.
- **かくしている (kakushite iru):** From <ruby>隠<rt>かく</rt></ruby>す (_kakusu_ — to hide / conceal). The progressive `-te iru` form denotes ongoing state ("holding you hidden away").
- **<ruby>灯<rt>ひ</rt></ruby> (hi):** Light, flame, or lamplight. Often written as <ruby>灯<rt>あか</rt></ruby>り (_akari_) or <ruby>灯火<rt>ともしび</rt></ruby> (_tomoshibi_), but sung here simply as _hi_. In Japanese imagery, town lights viewed from a ridge or hill signify warm human homes.
- **なつかしい (<ruby>懐<rt>なつ</rt></ruby>かしい - natsukashii):** Nostalgic, dear, evoking fond longing.
- **どれかひとつに (dore ka hitotsu ni):** In one of them / in some single one among many.

#### 💭 Poetic Nuance

The lyrical device **`〜のは〜からだ`** establishes a deeply personal worldview. Why does the evening horizon catch the eye? Not merely because of sunlight, but because somewhere beyond it, _you_ exist. Why do distant town lights stir affection? Because one of those hearths belongs to you.

### Stanza 2: The Departure & The Traveler’s Pack

| Japanese                                                                                                               | English                                                                                            |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| さあ　<ruby>出<rt>で</rt></ruby>かけよう　ひときれのパン<br>ナイフ　ランプ　かばんに<ruby>詰<rt>つ</rt></ruby>めこんで | Come, let us set out! Stowing a single slice of bread,<br>A knife, and a lamp tightly into the bag |

#### 🔑 Vocab & Points

- **さあ (saa):** "Come now!", "Well then!" — an encouraging interjection signaling a fresh beginning.
- **<ruby>出<rt>で</rt></ruby>かけよう (dekakeyou):** Volitional form of <ruby>出<rt>で</rt></ruby>かける (_dekakeru_ — to set out / depart). "Let's set off!"
- **ひときれ (<ruby>一切<rt>ひとき</rt></ruby>れ - hitokire):** One slice / a piece of (counter <ruby>切<rt>き</rt></ruby>れ for slices of bread, meat, fish).
- **<ruby>詰<rt>つ</rt></ruby>めこんで (tsumekonde):** Te-form of the compound verb <ruby>詰<rt>つ</rt></ruby>め<ruby>込<rt>こ</rt></ruby>む (_tsumekomu_ — to pack tightly, cram, or stuff into).

#### 💭 Poetic Nuance

This line vividly evokes Pazu in the mining town of Slag Ravine: simple, self-reliant, and unburdened. A single slice of bread, a pocketknife, and a miner’s carbide lamp are all the material gear needed for a grand adventure.

### Stanza 3: Legacy of Father & Mother

| Japanese                                                                                                                                                                                   | English                                                                      |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <ruby>父<rt>とう</rt></ruby>さんが<ruby>残<rt>のこ</rt></ruby>した　<ruby>熱<rt>あつ</rt></ruby>い<ruby>想<rt>おも</rt></ruby>い<br><ruby>母<rt>かあ</rt></ruby>さんがくれた　あのまなざし | The burning passion father left behind<br>That tender gaze mother gave to me |

#### 🔑 Vocab & Points

- **<ruby>残<rt>のこ</rt></ruby>した (nokoshita):** Past tense of <ruby>残<rt>のこ</rt></ruby>す (_nokosu_ — to leave behind, bequeath).
- **<ruby>熱<rt>あつ</rt></ruby>い<ruby>想<rt>おも</rt></ruby>い (atsui omoi):** Burning passion / fervent longing / conviction. Note the use of <ruby>想<rt>おも</rt></ruby>い (heartfelt thoughts, feelings, hopes) rather than just <ruby>思<rt>おも</rt></ruby>い.
- **まなざし (<ruby>眼差<rt>まなざ</rt></ruby>し - manazashi):** A look, gaze, or expression in one's eyes.

#### 💭 Poetic Nuance

Pazu is an orphan, but he is not empty-handed. His father left him the drive to explore and believe in the impossible (Laputa), while his mother gave him warmth, gentleness, and an observant eye. These inner inheritances guide him forward.

### Stanza 4: The Earth in Motion

| Japanese                                                                                                                                                                                 | English                                                                                  |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| <ruby>地球<rt>ちきゅう</rt></ruby>はまわる　<ruby>君<rt>きみ</rt></ruby>をかくして<br><ruby>輝<rt>かがや</rt></ruby>く<ruby>瞳<rt>ひとみ</rt></ruby>　きらめく<ruby>灯<rt>ひ</rt></ruby> | The earth turns, keeping you hidden<br>With shining eyes, and glittering lights          |
| <ruby>地球<rt>ちきゅう</rt></ruby>はまわる　<ruby>君<rt>きみ</rt></ruby>をのせて<br>いつかきっと<ruby>出会<rt>であ</rt></ruby>う　ぼくらをのせて                                         | The earth turns, carrying you along<br>Carrying both of us, who will surely meet one day |

#### 🔑 Vocab & Points

- **<ruby>地球<rt>ちきゅう</rt></ruby> (chikyuu):** The Earth / the globe.
- **まわる (<ruby>回<rt>まわ</rt></ruby>る - mawaru):** To rotate, spin, turn around.
- **<ruby>瞳<rt>ひとみ</rt></ruby> (hitomi):** Eyes, pupils (often used poetically to reflect purity and depth of soul).
- **きらめく (kirameku):** To glitter, twinkle, shimmer (vivid verb synonymous with glowing brightly).
- **のせて (<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>せて - nosete):** Te-form of the transitive verb <ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>せる (_noseru_ — to give a ride to, place atop, carry aboard).
- **<ruby>出会<rt>であ</rt></ruby>う (deau):** To meet, encounter (especially by fate or for the first time).
- **ぼくら (bokura):** We / us (masculine, youthful, friendly).

#### 💭 Poetic Nuance

The contrast shifts from intimate items (bread, a bag) to cosmic scale (<ruby>地球<rt>ちきゅう</rt></ruby>). The Earth’s rotation isn’t an indifferent astronomical fact—it is carrying two wandering children toward their destiny. First it is `君をかくして` (hiding you), but as it turns, it becomes `ぼくらをのせて` (bearing both of us together).

## 📖 Key Japanese Grammar & Expressions

### 1. The Explanatory Pattern: 〜のは〜から(だ)

This pattern nominalizes a statement with **のは** and gives the underlying reason or emotional motivation with **から**.

- <ruby>地平線<rt>ちへいせん</rt></ruby>が<ruby>輝<rt>かがや</rt></ruby>く (The horizon glows)  
  $\rightarrow$ <ruby>地平線<rt>ちへいせん</rt></ruby>が<ruby>輝<rt>かがや</rt></ruby>く**のは**、<ruby>君<rt>きみ</rt></ruby>をかくしている**から**  
  _"The reason the horizon glows is because it hides you."_
- Useful in daily Japanese:
  - <ruby>日本<rt>にほん</rt></ruby>へ<ruby>行<rt>い</rt></ruby>きたい**のは**、アニメが<ruby>好<rt>す</rt></ruby>きだ**から**です。 (_The reason I want to go to Japan is because I like anime._)

### 2. Transitive vs. Intransitive: 乗る (noru) vs. 乗せる (noseru)

- **<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>る (noru - intransitive):** Subject rides / boards something.
  - <ruby>電車<rt>でんしゃ</rt></ruby>に<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>る (_To ride the train_).
- **<ruby>乗<rt>の</rt></ruby>せる (noseru - transitive):** Subject carries / puts someone or something aboard.
  - <ruby>地球<rt>ちきゅう</rt></ruby>が<ruby>君<rt>きみ</rt></ruby>を**のせる** (_The Earth carries you aboard_).
  - <ruby>車<rt>くるま</rt></ruby>に<ruby>荷物<rt>にもつ</rt></ruby>をのせる (_To load luggage into the car_).

### 3. Compound Verb: 〜込む (こむ - komu)

Attaching **〜込む** to a verb stem indicates movement into an interior space, or performing an action thoroughly/deeply.

- **<ruby>詰<rt>つ</rt></ruby>める** (to pack, pinch, fill) + **込む** (inward) $\rightarrow$ **<ruby>詰<rt>つ</rt></ruby>め<ruby>込<rt>こ</rt></ruby>む** (_to cram / stuff tightly into_).
- Other common examples:
  - **<ruby>飛<rt>と</rt></ruby>び<ruby>込<rt>こ</rt></ruby>む** (_tobikomu_ — to dive in / jump into)
  - **<ruby>吸<rt>す</rt></ruby>い<ruby>込<rt>こ</rt></ruby>む** (_suikomu_ — to inhale / suck in)
  - **<ruby>思<rt>おも</rt></ruby>い<ruby>込<rt>こ</rt></ruby>む** (_omoikomu_ — to be convinced / have an ingrained impression)

### 4. Counters: 一切れ (ひと・きれ)

- Counter **<ruby>切<rt>き</rt></ruby>れ** (_-kire_) counts slices cut from a larger whole.
  - ひときれのパン = a slice of bread
  - ふたきれのピザ = two slices of pizza
  - ひときれのケーキ = a piece of cake
