**KBTG Techtopia 2026** (16 Sep 2026, Bangkok) is KBTG's annual technology festival. This year's talks kept returning to one question: as AI gets more capable, **what should stay human, and how do we stay human while using it?**

## Session Notes

### AI, business and the economy

- **[[Thailand Today - Building a Runway Towards the Future|Thailand Today: Building a Runway Towards the Future]]**  
  Deputy PM Suphajee Suthumpun's opening keynote: the three Ds (decoupling, diversification, decarbonisation), the K-shaped economy, the visitor economy and ASEAN's DEFA.
- **[[ROI or RIP - Beyond the Pilot Phase|ROI or RIP: Beyond the Pilot Phase]]**  
  Krating Poonpol on getting enterprise AI past the pilot: the 10/20/70 formula, onboarding AI to company context, DRIs, and building an AI-native company.

### Designing human-AI interaction

- **[[Prompts Wont Save Us|Prompts Won't Save Us: Human-AI Experiences Beyond Chatboxes]]**  
  Why the chat box is a poor interface: AI that gathers context like a good barber, reframes problems like an expert, and amplifies your taste instead of flattening it.
- **[[Cyborg Psychology|Cyborg Psychology: How Will AI Change How We Live, Love, and Learn?]]**  
  MIT Media Lab research on Future You, AI companionship and loneliness, the AI Nutrition Label, and shifting from AI to IA (intelligence augmentation).
- **[[The Intertwined Between Natural and Artificial Brain]]**  
  Prof. Chaipat Chunharas on AI for neurology (Parkinson's, speech, cognitive tests) and why AI is a mirror of human data, not a mind.

### Staying human

- **[[What Should Remain Deeply Human|What Should Remain Deeply Human?]]**  
  Roundfinger on stories, roots and the hedonic treadmill: using AI to reconnect, not disconnect.
- **[[Algebra of Luck|Algebra of Luck: Surviving as a Human in the AI Era]]**  
  K. Thana (Bluebik) on collecting dots (skills, experiences, people), skill stacking, and waking up with curiosity, discipline and empathy.
- **[[The Modern Wellness Crisis|The Modern Wellness Crisis: How to Be Kinder to Yourself]]**  
  Reading wearable data without judging yourself: trends over points, the two readings that mean see a doctor, and listening to your body first.

## Common Threads

**1. The bottleneck is human, not technical.** Krating puts 70% of AI transformation on people and culture; the design team argues the chat box pushes the burden onto users; Prof. Chaipat reminds us that AI only reflects the data humans gave it. Better models won't fix any of these on their own.

**2. Augment, don't replace.** MIT's "AI → IA", the designers' AI that reframes problems and teaches vocabulary, and K. Thana's dot-connecting all describe AI that makes the _person_ more capable. The warning in each is the reverse: AI that makes us think, love and create less.

**3. Judgement and taste become the differentiators.** "Good judgement will become scalable" (Krating), "it's so AI" as an insult (design team), and skill stacking that AI can't replicate (K. Thana) all point the same way: when output is cheap, what's scarce is knowing what's good.

**4. Don't let tools define you.** Roundfinger's hedonic treadmill, the wellness talk's "listen to your body before the device", and the documented loneliness from AI companions are the same failure mode. A tool that started as a mirror becomes the judge.

**5. Roots and relationships.** The most human talks ended in the same place: relationships, community and shared stories give life continuity, and the dying regret too little time with the people they loved. Use AI to win back time for those things.
