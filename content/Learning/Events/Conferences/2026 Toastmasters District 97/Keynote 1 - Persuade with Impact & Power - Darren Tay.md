# Keynote 1: Persuade with Impact & Power (Darren Tay)

![image](attachments/keynote_1_darren_tay_sketchnote.png)

_Darren Tay: World Champion of Public Speaking 2016._
**Express to Impress** book. Try selling it. Darren got a chance in high school to give a keynote and ended up selling 12,000 books.

## Pyramid of Persuasion

- **60% Unaware**: Need to know "Why I should buy" (Identity).
- **30% Interest**: How it’s different.
- **10% Active Buyer**: Ready to purchase.

## ANSVC Framework

### 1. Attention Grabber

Formula: **A + B - C**

- **A**: What audience wants (e.g., confident speaker).
- **B**: Time (e.g., in 3 months).
- **C**: What audience hates (phrase "without having to...").

**Techniques:**

- Audience survey question (Finish question first, then raise hands fully).
- Increase voice volume gradually through the sentence.
- Theoretical questions.
- Storytelling.

### 2. Needs

- **Pain**: What do they hope to solve?
- **FAQ**: Ask questions they don't even know they have.

### 3. Satisfaction & Solution

Convert features to benefits. Use phrases:

- "So that you may..."
- "In order for you to..."
- "To help you..."

### 4. Visualization

Sell the identity, not just the product.

- "Imagine yourself..."
- "Picture yourself..."
- "What if you could..."
- (e.g., Nike "Just Do It" sells the identity of an athlete).
- Visualize who you would become. "You become one of us."

### 5. Action

Be specific:

- Click to buy now.
- Follow up.
- Use limits (time, resources).
- Eliminate/reduce risk (e.g., 30-day money-back guarantee).
