# Keynote 2: Building Winning Habits That Last (Mas Mahathir)

![image](attachments/keynote_2_mas_mahathir_sketchnote.png)

*Speaker: Mas Mahathir, 2nd Place, 2025 World Champion of Public Speaking (WCPS).*

Ideas can only turn into impact through habits.

## Core Concepts
- **Microhabit**: It’s the small action that matters.
- **Atomic Habits (James Clear)**:
	- Small consistent actions can compound into transformation.
	- Don’t rely on motivation, build a system.

## The 4 Laws of Behavioral Change
To build identity-based habits, follow these laws:
1. **Make it Obvious**: Set a clear CUE for your new behavior.
2. **Make it Easy**: Start with small easy steps first.
3. **Make it Attractive**: Make the outcome meaningful enough to fight for.
4. **Make it Satisfying**: Find a way to reinforce the habit, through tracking/rewarding, to keep the habit consistent.

## Implementation Framework
### "Change it!" (Identity Shift)
Shift from wanting an outcome to becoming the person who produces it:
- **From**: "I want to be a World Champion speaker." → **To**: "I am a speaker who trains like a World Champion."
- **From**: "I want my team to be motivated." → **To**: "I am a leader who inspires action."
- **From**: "I want more members to renew their membership." → **To**: "I am a leader who creates so much value to my members."

### Identity-Based Habit Formula
"I am a **[Identity]** who **[Supporting Action]**, and over the next **[Timeframe]** days, I will **[Specific Action]** so that **[Outcome/Value]**."

**Examples:**
- "I am a business owner who makes decisions with clarity, courage, and consistency."
- "I am a leader who develops and empowers my team."
- "I am a Toastmaster who turns evaluations into action."
- "I am a speaker who trains like a World Champion."
- **Practical Application**: "I am a leader who **empowers others**, and over the next **30 days**, I will **recognize my team members when they take initiative**, so that **they continue to be proactive and motivated**."

## The Most Important Thing
**Desire**: It must be strong and clear.
- Habit Loop: Cue → Craving → Response → Reward.
