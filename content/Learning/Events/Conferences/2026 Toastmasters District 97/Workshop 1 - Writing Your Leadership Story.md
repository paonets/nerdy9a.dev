# Workshop 1: Writing Your Leadership Story (Poh Kim Siong)

![image](attachments/workshop_1_leadership_story_sketchnote.png)

**Speaker Profile: Poh Kim Siong, DTM, PID, PRA**
- Leadership & Honors: International Director (2008-2010), Region 13 Advisor (2020-2021), District 80 Governor (2006-2007)
- Highest Honor: 2019 Presidential Citation Award
- Global Impact: Trained leaders across 10+ countries
- Mentor & Inspirer: Ignites passion and mission in leaders worldwide.

> "Leadership is not a title, but influence. Public speaking is not technique, but resonance of the soul."
> *Write it, Share it, Live it.*

## Core Principles: Dao, Fa, Qi
### 1. Dao (The Core Truth or Principles)
Focus on the "Hot Button Issues" and mental models that drive leadership.
- **Mental Models**: How we work, research models, impact on design, fixing mismatches.
- **Dao**: Understanding the "why" and the fundamental truth behind your story.

### 2. Fa (Methods for Collecting Stories)
- **#01 Write It Down**: Capture ideas immediately.
- **Capture it**: Use notes, journals, or digital tools.
- **Use AI tools**: To help refine and structure.

### 3. Qi (The Tools and Skills)
The delivery of the story: **Voice + Movement + Emotion**.
- **Voice**: Tone, pace, and clarity.
- **Movement**: Gestures and stage presence.
- **Emotion**: Connecting with the audience's feelings.

## Story Structure
- **Scene**: Set the stage.
- **Tension**: Introduce the conflict.
- **Obstacle**: What stands in the way?
- **Rise**: The climax or turning point.
- **Your Lesson**: The leadership takeaway.

Blend **Audience + Message + Self** together using **Emotional Connection**.
