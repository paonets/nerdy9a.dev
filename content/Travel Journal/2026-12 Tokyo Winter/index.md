# Lights, Leaves & Wizards: Tokyo Winter 2026 (The Plan)

![image](attachments/tokyo_winter_sketchnote.webp)

> [!note] Pre-trip plan
> This is my plan before the trip. I'll turn it into a trip log with photos once I'm back.

## 🗺️ Overview

- **When:** Early December 2026, about a week
- **Base:** Tokyo, plus one day trip to Kamakura
- **Companions:** Solo
- **Themes:** Christmas lights, late autumn leaves, Harry Potter, anime and Pokémon, museums

## 📅 Day by Day

⭐ = the day's main highlight.

### Day 1: Arrival

- **Akihabara:** Tokyo's electronics, anime and game district. A quiet first evening browsing.

### Day 2: Akasaka, Yurakucho & Roppongi

- **Hie Shrine:** Hilltop shrine in Akasaka with a tunnel of red torii gates up a stairway.
- ⭐ **Harry Potter and the Cursed Child:** The stage sequel to the books, played in Japanese at its own theatre in Akasaka.
- **MAPPA EXPO:** Anniversary exhibition of MAPPA, the studio behind _Jujutsu Kaisen_ and _Chainsaw Man_.
- **Tokyo Midtown & Keyakizaka:** Roppongi's winter lights. Midtown has a lawn light show, and Keyakizaka is a street of trees lit in blue and white with Tokyo Tower behind.

### Day 3: Kamakura Autumn Leaves

Kamakura's maples turn late, usually peaking from late November into mid-December, so this is the trip's foliage day.

- **Kita-Kamakura temples:** Quiet Zen temples in wooded valleys, with red maples and golden ginkgo around the gates and gardens.
- **Great Buddha (Kōtoku-in):** A 13-metre bronze Buddha that has sat outdoors since the 1400s, framed by autumn trees.
- ⭐ **Hasedera:** Hillside temple with a maple-filled garden and pond, and a sea view from the terrace. One of Kamakura's best foliage spots.
- **Inamuragasaki:** Small cape for sunset over the sea, with Mt Fuji on clear days.
- **Shirasu:** Tiny whitebait, Kamakura's local speciality, served on rice.

### Day 4: Tokyo DisneySea

- ⭐ **Tokyo DisneySea:** Tokyo's sea-themed Disney park, open from rope drop to close.
- **Fantasy Springs:** The newest area, themed to _Frozen_, _Tangled_ and _Peter Pan_.
- **Christmas harbour shows:** Seasonal shows performed on the water.

### Day 5: PokéPark KANTO & Shinjuku

- ⭐ **PokéPark KANTO:** Permanent Pokémon outdoor park inside Yomiuriland amusement park, opened in 2026.
- **Shinjuku evening:** Puddings and dinner around the station.

### Day 6: Harry Potter Day

- ⭐ **Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo:** The Making of Harry Potter, with real sets, props and the Great Hall. In December it's dressed for _Hogwarts in the Snow_.
- **Yayoi Kusama Museum:** Small museum of the polka-dot artist's work, with timed entry.
- **Marunouchi Bright Holiday:** Free Christmas event in the office district by Tokyo Station. This year it's Harry Potter themed, with a Hogwarts Snow Tree, a Common Room pop-up and champagne-gold street lights.
- **Tokyo Station:** The red-brick 1914 station building, lit up at night.

### Day 7: Tsukiji, Comic Con & Toyosu

- **Tsukiji Outer Market:** Street food breakfast, from tamagoyaki to fresh seafood.
- **Tokyo Comic Con:** Japan's version of the big US comic and film fan convention.
- **teamLab Planets:** Walk-through digital art, part of it barefoot in water.
- ⭐ **Tim Burton's Labyrinth:** Walk-through exhibition of Tim Burton's art and films.

### Day 8: Art, Kabuki & Ginza

- **Tokyo National Museum:** Japan's oldest museum, showing Hiroshige's woodblock prints this time.
- **Sumida Hokusai Museum:** Museum of the _Great Wave_ artist in his home neighbourhood.
- **Azabudai Hills:** Tokyo's newest skyscraper and shopping complex.
- ⭐ **Kabukiza:** Ginza's kabuki theatre. Single-act tickets let you see one act of the traditional theatre.
- **Ginza:** Dinner in the city's upscale shopping district.

### Day 9: Fly Home

## 🎄 Why Early December

- **Late foliage:** Tokyo's maples and ginkgo peak around the end of November, and Kamakura's run into early to mid December.
- **Christmas lights everywhere:** Marunouchi's champagne-gold Naka-dori, Roppongi's Keyakizaka and Midtown, plus Christmas markets.
- **Free Harry Potter event:** Marunouchi Bright Holiday 2026 has a Hogwarts Snow Tree, a Common Room pop-up and themed shops.

## 💡 Planning Tips

- **Light-ups end early.** Most temple and garden foliage light-ups finish in the first week of December, but daytime colours last longer.
- **Motion sickness friendly:** At DisneySea and PokéPark I'm focusing on shows, gentle rides and walking.
- **Book ahead:** Studio Tour, teamLab, DisneySea and theatre tickets sell out. Free events like Marunouchi are walk-in.
- **Weekday evenings** are much calmer for the illuminations than weekends.
