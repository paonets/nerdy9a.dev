## 📝 Thoughts & Reflections

- The combination of sunset over tulip fields and night illumination was unforgettable.

---

## 📸 Journal & Photos

While in [[Travel_Journal/Places/Nagoya|Nagoya]], I made a side trip to [Nabana no Sato](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nabana+no+Sato+Kuwana), a stunning flower park that completely exceeded my expectations. 🌸🌷✨

In April, you get the rare chance to see sakura and tulips blooming at the same time. The tulip fields are massive, beautifully arranged, and look picture-perfect from every angle—especially during sunset, when the golden light makes the colors even more vivid.

After dark, the park transforms into a magical world with its famous night illumination. One of the highlights was a breathtaking light show featuring Mount Fuji, glowing in shifting colors and patterns.

If you’re visiting the Nagoya area in spring, Nabana no Sato is 100% worth the trip—day and night.
