## 🏨 Accommodations
- **[Nishitetsu Hotel Croom Nagoya](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nishitetsu+Hotel+Croom+Nagoya+Nagoya)** (Apr 6-11): Sakae base. Great onsen and location!
- **[Manseiro Ryokan](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Manseiro+Ryokan+Nachikatsuura)** (Apr 11-13): Old Ryokan in Katsuura. Free cave onsen ♨️ access at [Hotel Urashima](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hotel+Urashima+Nachikatsuura).
- **[Sanco Inn Iseshi-Ekimae Shikinoyu](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Sanco+Inn+Iseshi-Ekimae+Shikinoyu+Ise)** (Apr 13-14): Convenient station-front business hotel.
- **[Super Hotel Premier Osaka Honmachi Ekimae](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Super+Hotel+Premier+Osaka+Honmachi+Ekimae+Osaka)** (Apr 14-20): Osaka base. Subway adjacent, excellent onsen ♨️.
