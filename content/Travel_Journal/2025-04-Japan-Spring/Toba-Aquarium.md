# Toba: Coastal Transit & Aquarium

## 🗺️ Scope & Locations
- **Days / Dates:** April 13, 2025
- **Locations Covered:** [Toba Aquarium](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Toba+Aquarium+Toba), Toba Bay

---

## 📝 Thoughts & Reflections
- The dugong and giant isopods are unique highlights not found in most standard aquariums.
- Perfect indoor activity to escape a rainy day in the Mie/Ise area.

---

## 📸 Journal & Photos

On a rainy day in [[Travel_Journal/Places/Ise|Ise]], I hopped over to [Toba Aquarium](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Toba+Aquarium+Toba)—and wow, it’s huge! 🐠

The place is divided into 12 themed zones, including an outdoor area, so there’s plenty to explore at your own pace.

I got to see so many species I’d never seen before, like the gentle dugong and the alien-looking giant isopod. It’s also full of energetic kids enjoying the exhibits, which made the atmosphere lively and fun.

Japanese aquariums never disappoint—always well-designed, full of surprises, and just so much fun to visit. 🐬✨
