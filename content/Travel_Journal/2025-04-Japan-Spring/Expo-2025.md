# Osaka Expo 2025

## 🗺️ Scope & Locations
- **Days / Dates:** April 15 - April 19, 2025 (Expo visits), April 20 (Departure)
- **Locations Covered:** 
  - [Expo 2025 Osaka](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Expo+2025+Osaka+Yumeshima) (on [Yumeshima Island](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yumeshima+Island+Osaka))
  - [Kansai International Airport (KIX)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kansai+International+Airport)

---

## 📝 Thoughts & Reflections
- Staying in Honmachi was perfect—it allowed quick subway connections directly to the transit routes heading to Yumeshima for the Expo.
- Doing five days at the Expo was a lot of walking, but it allowed us to see most of the major pavilions without feeling rushed.

---

## 📸 Journal & Photos

We spent five full days exploring **[[Travel_Journal/Places/Osaka|Osaka]] [Expo 2025](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Expo+2025+Osaka+Yumeshima)** on the artificial island of [Yumeshima](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yumeshima+Island+Osaka). It was an incredible gathering of futuristic technology, architecture, and international pavilions. 

We stayed at the **[Super Hotel Premier Osaka Honmachi Ekimae](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Super+Hotel+Premier+Osaka+Honmachi+Ekimae+Osaka)** (detailed in [[Travel_Journal/2025-04-Japan-Spring/Osaka|Osaka City & Sights]]), which was highly convenient—right next to the subway entrance and featuring an excellent onsen and breakfast buffet.

### April 15 - 19: Expo Visits
We spent the days navigating the different zones, visiting international pavilions, and checking out the latest innovations in sustainability and design.

### April 20: Departure
We headed to **[Kansai International Airport (KIX)](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kansai+International+Airport)** and caught our flight back to Bangkok.
