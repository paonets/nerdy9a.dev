# Da Nang: Coastal Caves & Hoi An Lanterns

## 🗺️ Overview

- **Dates:** May 14 - May 17, 2026
- **Location:** Da Nang & Hoi An, Vietnam
- **Companions:** Solo

## 📝 Thoughts & Reflections

- **Midday Escape:** The afternoon heat in Da Nang in May is intense. Spending the hot hours inside air-conditioned spots like the [Da Nang Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Da+Nang+Museum+Da+Nang) or local cafes is highly recommended.
- **Hoi An Travel:** Grabbing a private car or Grab for the 45-minute drive to Hoi An is very convenient. The town is incredibly atmospheric at night with all the glowing silk lanterns.

---

## 📌 Places Visited (Single Day Highlights)

I visited all these main spots in a single day:

- **[Lady Buddha](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lady+Buddha+Da+Nang)** (Son Tra Peninsula): Morning visit for cool coastal air and panoramic sea views.
- **[Marble Mountains](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Marble+Mountains+Da+Nang)**: Hiked up the paths and walked inside the limestone chambers, especially the Huyen Khong cave.
- **[Da Nang Museum](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Da+Nang+Museum+Da+Nang)**: Explored local history and cultural exhibitions.
- **[Hoi An Ancient Town](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hoi+An+Ancient+Town)**: Explored the historic lantern-lit streets and went on a lantern boat ride.
